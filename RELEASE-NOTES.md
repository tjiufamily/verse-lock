Verse Lock v4.21

The defaults release, plus a more readable prayer.

What is new since 4.14:
* Short prayers are the new standard. Each prayer now shows its heart: the thanksgiving, the declaration, the settled confession and the surrender, so scrolling through verses reads as one continual prayer. Prefer the complete prayer with its opening worship and closing thanksgiving? Switch to Full under Prayer length in Personal. The choice is yours, both forms are in the app in all four languages.
* The prayer text is larger, brighter, and set in paragraphs that follow its movements, Short and Full alike.
* New installs start with the verse notification on, nature photo backgrounds, and a widget colour that follows the phone (System). Every choice you already made stays exactly as you set it.
* Share Verse Lock now sits in Settings, with a ready message carrying the Android and iPhone links, for passing the app to a friend.
* The widget colour picker is a clearer 2 by 2 grid.
* Same signing key as always, so this installs over your current version with your favorites and settings kept.
