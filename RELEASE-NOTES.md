Verse Lock v3.18

A small tidy up release.

What changed:
* The Send test result messages no longer mention any third party name. They simply report the result and, if something is wrong, a short code to note down.
* Everything from v3.17 and v3.16 stands: the notification carries the app icon (sword and shield), and it posts in the plain, reliable shape that lands every morning at 7:00 AM.
* Same signing key as always, so this installs over your current version with your favorites and settings kept.
