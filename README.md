# Verse Lock

Scripture on your lock screen. A fresh NKJV verse, a matching quote from a man of God, and an affirmation written for that verse, rotating through your day, on a nature photo background.

Built by James Tjiunardi, first for his own phone, and shared freely with friends.

**Latest version:** 3.19 · **Platform:** Android 8.0 and above · **Size:** about 12 MB · **Price:** free, no ads, no account

## What it does

* 780 NKJV verses across 13 categories, including Peace, Strength, Faith, Hope, and Healing and Health
* A quote under every verse, matched to its theme, from ten men of God: Billy Graham, Reinhard Bonnke, Smith Wigglesworth, Yonggi Cho, Derek Prince, Charles Spurgeon, A. W. Tozer, Hudson Taylor, George Muller, William Carey
* An affirmation for every verse, and the option to write your own words instead
* 29 moods: tell it how you feel and it ministers to that exact feeling
* My own verse: pin a verse you type yourself on your lock screen
* Rotation every 1, 3, 6, 12, or 24 hours, a verse of the day notification with the full verse text, and home and lock screen widgets with Previous and Next controls
* 31 nature photo backgrounds, one for each day of the month
* Favorites, sharing a verse as an image, and your name woven into the affirmations

## Install on Android

1. Download the APK from the link above.
2. Tap the downloaded file. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install apps this once. This is the standard Android step for any app installed outside the Play Store.
3. Tap Install, then Open.
4. Google Play Protect may say it does not recognize the developer. That is expected for a personal app shared directly like this. Choose Install anyway. You can verify the file first using the checksum below.

Updating later works the same way: download the newer APK and install it over the old one. Your favorites and settings carry over, because every release is signed with the same key.
